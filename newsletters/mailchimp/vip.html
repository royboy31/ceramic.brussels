The Hoxton ●

Set just above the Botanical Gardens and a short walk from Brussels’ historic centre, The Hoxton is our new hotel partner — a design-led address with bold, 70s-inspired interiors and sweeping views over the city skyline. Beyond its rooms, it’s also a lively meeting point, from Cantina Valentina’s Peruvian-inspired plates to Tope’s rooftop terrace.
