Destination Basel

After 3 widely acclaimed editions, ceramic brussels is taking a monu­mental leap forward. In June 2027, we are heading to Switzerland during the world’s most prestigious art week to launch a brand-new fair: ceramic basel | celebrating fire & māteria.
JUNE 2027
||||||||||||||||||||||||||||

Every summer, Basel Art Week turns the city into the epicentre of the contemporary art market. By launching ceramic basel during this key moment in the art calendar, we are taking the fair to an international level, offering galleries, artists and collectors an unparalleled global platform.

This new chapter also comes with a broader, ambitious perspective. The fair’s inaugural edition will celebrate not only ceramics, but also glass, textile and, more broadly, the arts of fire and matter. ceramic basel is conceived as a living manifesto that places artistic gesture, savoir-faire and material experimentation at the heart of the art market, while keeping the signature DNA and bold scenography of ceramic brussels.
WHAT'S ON
||||||||||||||||||||||||||||

Across 4,000 m2 at the iconic Messe and Congress Center Basel, ceramic basel will feature two main curated sections:

fire ●
dedicated to modern, contemporary & historical ceramics, glass, and the arts of fire
māteria ■
open to all other forms of sculptural creation, from textile to bronze and wood

AND ALSO…
ceramic basel | celebrating fire & māteria will also feature a rich cultural programme, including:

↘ a tribute — conceived as a solo homage to a master artist whose work has significantly shaped art history and the practice of the arts of fire

↘ curated spaces & projects — developed in close collaboration with leading international institutions and museums

↘ talks & conferences — a vibrant platform for dialogue with artists, curators and experts.
