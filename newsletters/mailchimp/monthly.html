Main focus
interview with Marion Verboom ●

She is French, a sculptor, and works with clay, glass and many other materials… and above all, she will be the guest of honour of ceramic brussels 2027: Marion Verboom.

She opened the doors of her studio to talk to us about her creative process, the place of clay and drawing in her work, and the exhibition she is preparing for Tour & Taxis next January, in collaboration with Galerie Lelong Paris.

meet Anaïs Sandra Carion, CEO of MAD Brussels ●

Anaïs Sandra Carion has worked closely with ceramic brussels since its earliest days. With her sharp eye on the contemporary scene and her drive to break down boundaries between disciplines, she weaves original connections each year between ceramics, design, and Brussels’ creative scene. This year, the MAD team is going even further, reimagining our brand-new VIP lounge: a space for meeting and exchange bringing together a carefully curated selection of creations by Belgian designers, through sensory experiences that invite visitors to discover design in a whole new way.

Partner spotlight
IAC — International Academy of Ceramics ●

In 2027, ceramic brussels will celebrate the 75th anniversary of the International Academy of Ceramics (IAC) with a special programme. On 22 January, two talks will focus in particular on the future of the Academy, followed by an exclusive gathering for its members invited to the fair.

Founded in 1952 and based at the Musée Ariana in Geneva, the IAC is a global organisation dedicated to ceramic art and culture. It brings together more than 1,200 members from 92 countries: artists, curators, collectors, museums and schools. Guided by the belief that clay is a universal material that connects people and cultures, the Academy fosters exchange between ceramic communities worldwide.

All eyes on Basel
ceramic basel: next chapter ●

As you may already know, the first edition of ceramic basel will take place from 15 to 20 June 2027. Built on a shared vision and a strong complementarity with ceramic brussels, the fair will bring together around 65 leading international galleries at the heart of the most influential week in the art world, united by one engagement: celebrating fire and māteria.

The fair will unfold in two sections:

fire ●
celebrating the arts of ceramics and glass
 
māteria ■

what the team warmly recommends this month ●
→ Ceramics, Sensitive Bodies

The neck of a vase, its belly, its foot, its shoulders: these are the words used to describe a form shaped by the potter’s hand, as one would describe a human body… Bringing together 80 works from different periods and regions of the world, this exhibition, which marks the reopening of the MADD Bordeaux, highlights the unique relationship between the body and ceramic technique. Video archives complete the visit, taking us to the heart of the workshops.
