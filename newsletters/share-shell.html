The four newsletters in live text

Félicie's newsletters rebuilt as Mailchimp templates: real text instead of pictures of text, the same designs, one column on a phone. Pick a newsletter below. The VIP code is a sample; each guest gets their own.

Compared with the image versions

  • Readable with images off, so they no longer look like spam. Untick "Images on" to see.
  • Same layouts, colours, texts and photos. The animated GIFs, the video still and the logos stay images, as in the originals.
  • Two columns on a computer, one on a phone, without separate mobile versions.

What Félicie can edit in Mailchimp

  • Every text, picture and button: click it and change it.
  • Every row can be duplicated or hidden.
  • Colours and font sizes in the Design tab.
